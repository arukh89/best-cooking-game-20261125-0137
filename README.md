Dapur Kilat — Rush Kitchen: a complete cooking game. Orders pile up on the board; pick ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the board, cook them on the stove, build each dish on the assembly plate, and ring the bell to serve before the order's timer runs out. Five original recipes, combo tips, a rush hour, and a day timer. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best cooking game” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with keyboard and mouse: WASD / arrows or click to move, E or Space to interact, 1-6 to pick ingredients, M to mute.
